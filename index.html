01

Research

My work sits at the intersection of robotics, physics, and biology. I use experiments and models to uncover how embodiment can simplify control and improve locomotion in complex environments.

01

Compliant locomotion

How body elasticity and passive dynamics shape efficient swimming and terrestrial movement.

02

Robust control

Feedback and geometric strategies for reliable multi-legged locomotion on uncertain terrain.

03

Bio-inspired design

Soft actuators and tendon-coupled mechanisms that translate biological principles into machines.
